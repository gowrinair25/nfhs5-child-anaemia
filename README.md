# Child anaemia across Indian districts (NFHS-5)

**Question:** Which child, maternal and household factors are associated with anaemia in Indian children aged 6–59 months, how much of the variation sits at district vs state level, and does gradient boosting predict anaemia in unseen districts better than a multilevel model?

Work in progress.

## Data
Uses the India National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5, 2019–21) from the DHS Program. The data cannot be redistributed and is not in this repository. To reproduce, register at https://dhsprogram.com, request India 2019–21, and put the Children's Recode (Stata, `IAKR7EDT.ZIP`) unzipped into `data/raw/`.

Citation: IIPS and ICF. 2021. *National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5), 2019-21: India.* Mumbai: IIPS.
